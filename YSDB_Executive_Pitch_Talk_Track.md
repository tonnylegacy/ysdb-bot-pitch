---

### Objection 2: *"Why not just do Copy Trading or a PAMM account instead of building software?"*
* **Core Concern**: Thinks copy trading is simpler to set up.
* **The Response**:
  > *"Copy trading sounds good in theory, but in practice on Gold, it is a technical disaster for three reasons:*
  > 
  > 1. ***Broker Execution Latency***: *If you execute on Broker A, by the time the copy bridge pushes the trade to a user on Broker B or C, the market has moved 5–10 pips. In Gold scalping, that slippage completely destroys profitability.*
  > 2. ***Lot-Size Rounding Disasters***: *If our master account is $50,000 and a client has $500, fractional lot rounding either over-leverages them or fails to execute entirely.*
  > 3. ***Regulatory Exposure***: *Managing pooled funds or running copy trading puts you directly in the crosshairs of financial regulators requiring asset management licenses. Selling automated execution software keeps us cleanly in the FinTech software provider space.*
  > 
  > *Local EA execution means the bot runs right on the client's MT5. Zero bridge lag, zero slippage, zero broker conflicts."*
